If you’ve been following this blog for a while, you’ve been with me on some wild rides. You were there when I took my first leap into the unknown, leaving Barcelona for a [one-way trip to Latin America](https://nomad.danieleghidoli.it/2018/03/24/adventure-digital-nomad-latin-america-wifitribe/). You’ve seen me [pet alligators in Bolivia](https://nomad.danieleghidoli.it/2019/04/05/amazing-things-one-year-travel-latin-america), hike glaciers in [Patagonia](https://nomad.danieleghidoli.it/2018/11/08/patagonia-journey-penguins-glaciers-blue-lakes), and get lost in the beautiful chaos of [Havana](https://nomad.danieleghidoli.it/2019/01/09/living-like-local-havana/).

From the outside, it probably looked like I was just a guy chasing adventure, ticking off a bucket list. And in a way, I was. But I was also chasing something else, something I couldn’t quite put my finger on. I was running a huge, unplanned experiment.

For seven years, across more than 30 countries, I wasn’t just collecting passport stamps. I was collecting data. I was on a quest to figure out what actually makes us happy. And now, I’ve taken everything I learned and put it into my new book: **_The Travel Happiness Method: The Science-Backed System for Designing Joy Anywhere._**

![](~/assets/images/happiness-book-cover.jpg)

This isn’t your typical travel guide. I’m not going to give you a list of the “Top 10 Beaches in Costa Rica” or tell you how to pack a suitcase. This book is about something deeper. It’s the story of how travel became my accidental happiness laboratory.

It's a book filled with the real stories from my journey—the kind I don't always share on the blog. You'll discover how the moments when travel plans unravel often become the most fertile ground for profound human connection. You'll see how a single, spontaneous 'yes' to a stranger can lead you across the invisible line that separates a tourist from a welcome guest, transforming a simple trip into a lesson on what it truly means to be welcomed into another's world.

For years, these were just experiences and feelings. The real ‘aha!’ moment came when I discovered **Dr. Laurie Santos’s “The Science of Well-Being”** course from Yale. It was like someone had handed me the instruction manual for my own life. Her course gave me the scientific language to understand *why* these travel experiences were so transformative. It confirmed that my accidental discoveries weren't just flukes; they were real, evidence-based principles for a happier life. This book connects the dots between my adventures on the road and the proven science of what truly makes us flourish.

Most importantly, this book is about what happens *after* the journey. It’s about how you can take the lessons learned on the road—the courage, the gratitude, the deep human connections—and use them to consciously design a life you love, right where you are. It’s about building a life you don’t need a vacation from.

This book is the culmination of my biggest adventure yet. It's for the aspiring traveler, the seasoned nomad, and anyone who feels a little stuck and is curious about how to find more joy in their daily life.

I’m so excited to finally share it with you.
